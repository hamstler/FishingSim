# FishingSim

Original RF4-style fishing simulator, Godot 4.3 .NET (C#). Polish README in README.md.

## Build / test
- Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later for the whole project (LICENSE; README section "Licencja" lists the third-party parts). Exports: `export_presets.cfg` (Windows, Linux) include `assets/fish/*` and exclude tools/tests/docs; the .glb models carry `importer="keep"` .import files (never a .gdignore in assets/fish: exports skip such folders).
- `dotnet build` (Godot.NET.Sdk 4.3.0; in offline sandboxes restore via the bundled GodotSharp/Tools/nupkgs source).
- Tests: `FISHSIM_NO_SAVE=1 godot --headless --path . res://tests/run_tests.tscn -- <suite>` (core, accounts, progress, world, game, tournaments, village, gear).
- Screenshots: `tests/screenshot.tscn` (use xvfb-run + `--rendering-driver vulkan` with lavapipe, ~30 s per shot; volumetric fog needs Forward+).

## Architecture
Pure logic in src/Core (injected IRng); data as .tres resources; saves via System.Text.Json.Nodes; shaders in shaders/.

## Art direction
- One look only: Nordic (Valheim-like). Native resolution in the root viewport, crisp procedural textures, volumetric fog with sun shafts, AO, bloom, teal-shadow/warm-light grade (GraphicsQuality.ApplyNordic), moonlit ambient at night (SkyController). Pixel art was removed.
- Textures (everything but the fish): 4096 px per 2 m tile (ground, bark, buildings; leaf atlas 4096 with 2048 cells, needles 2048, fern 1024x4096), painted on worker threads (GroundTextures records strokes and paints them in 32 parallel bands; designs are in 1024-px units scaled by K) and block compressed by the game itself (Graphics/BlockCompress: BC1 colour, BC3 colour+alpha, BC5 normal RG: Godot's compressors exist only in the editor), cached zstd-compressed in user://texcache/*.fstx (Graphics/TextureStore; bump the owner's CacheVersion/LeafVersion when a design changes; stale versions and old PNGs are deleted). Menu starts painting them in the background (GroundTextures/Textures/ProceduralTextures Prewarm*); headless runs skip them. Trilinear anisotropic filtering (BuildingFactory.Pixel). Window starts maximised, canvas_items stretch, 3D at native resolution. Fish skins: src/Graphics/FishSkin paints them per pixel on worker threads (512×256 for fish in the lake, 4096×2048 + normal map for hooked/held/atlas fish via FishModel.Create(..., detailed: true)); screenshot mode 20 = skin sheet, 19 = atlas. Decor: pier festoon lights (Scenery.BuildStringLights), golden fish signs, Graphics/Gulls.
- Photographed CC0 materials (the user wants everything free for commercial use: only CC0 sources): Poly Haven sets in assets/textures/<id>/ (diff.webp, nor.webp = OpenGL normal, disp.webp for grounds, info.json with the real size), fetched by `python3 -I tools/assets/polyhaven.py [--grey] [--disp] ids` (hosts api.polyhaven.com, dl.polyhaven.org; CREDITS.md rewritten), "keep" .import files (the game decodes and block-compresses them itself: Graphics/PhotoTextures, cached in texcache). Used when present, painted designs otherwise: BuildingFactory.Photo(id, tint) (walls triplanar at the set's metres, roofs by UV in metres), ground layers (GroundTextures.Photos, 2048), bark (TreeFactory.BarkMaterial(kind): pine_bark / bark_brown_02, painted birch), pier deck. Geometry has soft edges: Shapes.RoundedBox replaces BoxMesh in BuildingFactory.Box; log cabins are real logs (Shapes.Log, LogWalls); roofs are thick rounded slabs. CC0 props (Poly Haven, `--model`): assets/models/<id>.glb (+ .json) with WebP pictures, loaded by World/Props/PropAsset (GltfDocument for meshes, pictures decoded and compressed by the game, ORM channels), used by Barrel, Crates, Lamp/Lantern, Campfire (stone_fire_pit), Well bucket, Goods baskets, Woodpile stump; headless runs fall back to primitives. Villagers and the angler's arms: Quaternius's free Standard packs (CC0; the user rejected his low-poly "Ultimate Modular" people as not realistic enough): Universal Base Characters (heads, eyes, hair), Modular Character Outfits Fantasy (Peasant, Ranger), Universal Animation Library 1+2. `python3 -I tools/assets/quaternius.py [--src DIR]` fetches them from itch.io like the "No thanks, just take me to the downloads" link (hosts quaternius.itch.io, itch.io, itchio-mirror.cb031a832f44726753d6267436f3b414.r2.cloudflarestorage.com) and writes assets/characters/: <model>.glb (male/female peasant/ranger: outfit parts + the base body cut to the head and the upper chest under the collar (pulled 1 cm in) + eyes, brows, every hairstyle of that sex as its own mesh, all re-bound to the outfit skeleton (65 UE-style bones, bones along local +Y, T-pose with palms down), tangents computed, surfaces named skin/eye/hair1/hair2/outfit, no pictures), anims_male/anims_female.glb (23 animations retargeted per bone relative to the rest pose, only the pelvis keeps translation, scaled), textures/*.webp (decoded and BC-compressed by the game, cached, prewarmed from the menu), <model>.json (pictures per material incl. two outfit colourings, hair meshes). World/Props/CharacterAsset.Create(Appearance, idle) instantiates a PackedScene per model, sets materials (skin SSS, double-sided cloth and hair, ORM), shows one hairstyle/beard/hood, adds an AnimationPlayer with the shared library; HeadLook (SkeletonModifier3D) turns neck_01+Head to the camera. People.Dress picks model, appearance, idle (FoldArms, Talking, Farm_*) and gestures per Role; Npc.Rig plays them, nods ("Yes") at the angler, stops animating beyond 70 m. Fishing/FirstPersonBody shows only the peasant's "Arms" mesh with its eyes at the camera (leaning 15 cm forward) and World/Props/ArmIK (SkeletonModifier3D: two-bone IK to a wrist target with a pole, hand frame from fingers/palm directions, half the twist into the forearm, finger curl by Grip) holds the rod handle (GripHandle/GripPalm/GripFingers in rod-rig space) and the reel knob (the landed fish is not held: the user found it artificial); FISHSIM_IK_DEBUG=1 prints the solve. Headless runs keep the primitive figures and built hands. Screenshot modes 23 (studio lineup: arg 0 roles, 1 models in both colourings, 3 head close-up) and 24 (in front of villager ARG, FISHSIM_SHOT_DIST).
- Ground and nature materials (no toon lighting anywhere): World/Props/GroundTextures paints seven seamless 2 m layers (grass, forest floor, sand, path, lake bed, rock, snow; 1024 px, height in the albedo's alpha, normal map RG = tilt, B = cavity, A = roughness) into Texture2DArrays (albedo BC3), cached in user://texcache (bump its CacheVersion). LakeBed.GroundAt writes the weights into the terrain mesh's UV (sand, path) and UV2 (forest floor, bed); shaders/terrain.gdshader height-blends the layers (borders wander by noise), rock by slope/altitude (triplanar), snow by altitude, two rotated readings against tiling, the map's colours via layer_tint (each layer's mean becomes the map's colour, meadows slightly calmed). Boulders: TreeFactory.Boulder + shaders/rock.gdshader (the rock layer, triplanar, moss on tops). Trunks: TreeFactory.Trunk (root flare) + shaders/bark.gdshader with GroundTextures.Bark (furrowed, or birch). Leaves: ProceduralTextures.LeafCluster = 2x2 atlas (oak, birch, willow, hazel; LeafCards' atlas cell) or needle sprays, FernFrond for ferns; the crown depth rides in the vertex alpha. Flowers: TreeFactory.Wildflower + shaders/flower.gdshader. Two-sided grass/foliage/floating shaders undo the engine's back-face normal flip (`if (!FRONT_FACING) NORMAL = -NORMAL;`) or half the blades/leaves go black. Clouds: soft cumulus lit through three steps towards the sun (sky.gdshader). Headless runs skip painting the ground and bark.
- Performance: never set sky material params or move the sun every frame (re-bakes radiance); cloud drift is the global shader param `cloud_drift`. Profile with FISHSIM_PERF=1 and screenshot mode 13.
- UI look: src/UI/UiArt paints the interface (carved oak nine-patch panels with brass studs, planked buttons incl. gilded "gold" ones via UITheme.Primary, parchment, spotlight, gilded bars, coin/lock/owned/star badges); UITheme.Make uses it for every screen. The shop has a category rail (TabContainer kept for tests, TabsVisible=false), sub-category chips (Shop.Subcategories), search/sort/"Dostępne", tier ribbons by RequiredLevel, hover/appear/pop tweens, coin burst + OneShot.Coins on purchase (FishingRod.Cue). Icons are painted on a 4x canvas (smooth shading, soft outline, drop shadow) and saved at 128 px.
- UI pictures: src/Graphics/Icons (items, fish), src/Graphics/Postcard (one composition per map). Screenshot modes 16 (icon sheet), 17 (postcards) and 18 (top-down map shapes) run headless.
- Villages: LocationData.VillageStyle (timber/farm/fishing/mountain -> BuildingFactory.Style: walls, roofs, shutters, fences, villager roles) and Streets (door paths go to the nearest street); kinds Cabin, RedHouse, StoneHouse, Smokehouse, Tent, MarketTent, Stall, Well, Woodpile, DryingRack, Cart, Villager (BuildingFactory.Village.cs); every functional building gets its keeper (AddKeeper). LakeBed.VillageGround eases the village to the square's height (MapLayout.PlazaHeight) so pads do not make mounds.
- Maps are unique via LocationData Squareness/Lobes/Islands (LakeBed.InsideMetres) and Landmarks (World/Props/Landmarks.cs).
- Relief and map edge: LakeBed.NaturalHeight adds HillsAt (domes in 46 m cells, at most 0.45 of their radius high, faded off the beach and around the village via Unflattened) and RimAt (mountains rising from the forest belt to LocationData.RimHeight with ridges; SnowLine/RockLine per map feed terrain.gdshader). The playable map is LakeBed.XMin..ZMax (also the minimap area); its edge is PlayDistance (rounded rectangle, wobbling) and IsWalkable stops the angler there, inside the dense rim forest (Scenery.ScatterTrees: 3.4 m jittered grid in the belt, sparser up the slopes to the tree line). The terrain mesh is a variable grid (GridLines: 2.5 m over the map + DetailMargin, widening to 14 m out to Outer) with collision only over the map. Trees are drawn per 64 m cell twice (Scenery.AddTrees): full near TreeNearRange, TreeFactory.GetFar versions (fewer, larger cards, 6-sided trunks) beyond.
- Foliage (Scenery: ScatterTrees records tree spots for the understorey of hazel + ferns; ScatterGroundCover, ScatterReeds, ScatterShoreline = rushes + yellow irises, ScatterWaterPlants = lily/pondweed patches by LocationData.LilyDensity, kept off the pier's water): small plants go through AddMultiMeshChunked (one MultiMeshInstance3D per 36-48 m cell with a visibility range scaled by GraphicsQuality.FoliageRange), no shadows for grass/reeds. Floating leaves use shaders/floating.gdshader riding the water's waves via the global shader params water_time/water_amp (set in Water.PushParameters) and float ~0.45 amplitudes up (the 2 m water mesh strays from the exact waves). Godot front faces are clockwise.
- No GPUParticles: rain is a MultiMesh + shaders/rain.gdshader (GPU particles with motion vectors crashed Godot 4.3 when two viewports drew them).
- Rod: src/Core/Physics/RodBendModel drives RodRig.SetJointAngles (RodData.Action).
- Quality presets in src/Graphics/GraphicsQuality (pause menu button): High (default) = SSAO + volumetric fog + TAA; SSIL, SSR, MSAA and 4 shadow splits only on Ultra; SDFGI off.
- Fish models: src/Fishing/FishBody (per-species build table, iris colours, mouth size) + FishModel (lofted with a rounded nose cap, cached per species at unit length, scaled). Close-up (detailed) fish use shaders/fish_body + fish_fin (one swimming wave from fish_bend.gdshaderinc, instance uniforms wiggle_amount/swim_phase), painted fins (Graphics/FinPainter: rays, membrane, markings), iris discs (EyePainter) under a cornea; head details (mouth, nostrils, gill cover, eye socket) are painted into FishSkin. Screenshot mode 22 = studio close-up (arg = species index, also writes *_head.png). Screenshot mode 21 = side views of 20 species per page (arg = page). Light skins are pre-painted in parallel when a lake is stocked.
- Fish references: the user rejected photo-textured fish ("too photographic"); fish stay painted and game-like, but species with assets/fish/<id>.json (FishReference) are lofted to the side-view profile and painted in the back/flank/belly colours measured from a freely licensed Wikimedia Commons photo (tools/fish_photos: fetch.py search/previews/more via commons thumb.php, cutout.py with --isnet [ISNet ONNX model, tool-only], measure.py, finalize.py; photos are not shipped, CREDITS.md lists them). FishModel.Growth/Proportions: heavier fish are deeper and thicker, young ones slimmer with bigger eyes; the landed fish is shown on UI/CatchPanel: full-screen dim + carved card, a SubViewport studio (own world, transparent over a spotlight, lights only) where FishingRod.HeldFishModel (made hidden under the camera, adopted by the panel, freed by the rod once decided) turns slowly about its vertical axis after spinning in, chips (weight, length = FishModel.Length, value, XP, record), warnings, E/R buttons; the stage height follows the screen (fits 720p). FISHSIM_SHOT_WEIGHT sets the weight and FISHSIM_SHOT_WAIT the seconds to let it turn in screenshot mode 2.
- Species models (all 66): tools/fish_model. measure_atlas.py measures a side-view illustration (AtlasRyb.online, measurements only: body outline without fins via steep-edge fin spans + asymmetric smoothing, then a second pass that bridges the back/belly under each found fin with margins for its short first and overhanging last rays, so no hump at the fin (Long dorsals of carp/crucian end at the last steep drop before the stalk); fins and eye are given in the snout-stalk axis frame like the outline; each fin's base and edge, the eye by a pupil-in-iris matched filter at several sizes with the table's position as a prior (EYE_HINT/EYE_SMALL for pictures that defeat it), colour bands, fin colours; CROPS picks one fish on pictures with two) -> compose_species.py joins it with the game's tables (FISHSIM_DUMP_FISH=<file> in the gear suite dumps FishBody/skin style/iris/mouth) and the perch's head anatomy (species/okon.json is the hand-tuned template, mapped by snout/eye/gill-cover landmarks) into species/<id>.json (fin bases snapped onto the outline); relatives without a picture borrow one (REFERENCE/ADJUST tables), curled or missing fish (sum, wegorz, sumiki, glowacze, babka) are built from FishBody envelopes. fishgen.py smooths the outline (monotone cubic, snout made convex over its first 0.04, a rounded nose cap as long as the snout's front is broad), sculpts the mouth in proportion to the gape (ML: lips, crease running through the snout's tip, rings kept from folding) and paints textures at 4096x2048 for every species (normal map at full size, fins 1024x512; unwrapped skin with overlapping scales (thin dark edge + light middle, contrast by scale size, growth rings, radii and edge pigment close up; cheek scales only for perches) or smooth skin, patterns: bars/saddles/light_spots/red_spots/dark_spots/rainbow/vermiculated/marbled/stripe(s)/spot_rows/band/scutes/mirror, head anatomy, normal map; fins; iris; barbels) and writes the lake outline (--reference). fish_blender.py (blender -b) builds the glb with WebP pictures (EXT_texture_webp, quality 85, encoded once from fishgen's lossless PNGs: smaller and truer than JPEG then WebP; quad body 190x128, Solidify fins with ray tubes, Mirror paired fins, barbel tubes, eyes in a socket 0.45 eye radii deep so the dome rises out of its floor beyond the iris (the skin never cuts the iris), turned to face as the head's surface does, under clear corneas, the iris metallic to its rim with a dark edge, darker above; stickleback spines are free needles to each measured tip). tools/fish_model/build_all.sh rebuilds everything (Blender 4.0 + python3-numpy from apt; the environment's setup script installs them). FishAsset loads assets/fish/models/<id>.glb at run time (GltfDocument for meshes; the pictures are decoded by FishAsset itself with mipmaps and S3TC-compressed (BC1/BC3, BC5 for the normal map; FISHSIM_RAW_FISH_TEXTURES=1 keeps them plain), on a worker thread via FishAsset.Prefetch when a fish nibbles; at most 6 species stay cached); FishModel.BuildAsset uses it for close-up fish, with shaders/fish_eye + fish_cornea so the eyes sway with the head. FISHSIM_SHOT_SIZE=WxH sets the screenshot size.

## Game rules worth knowing
- Tackle (data/tackle/*, lists in TackleLoadout and GearCatalog): RodKind/LineKind/BaitKind; TackleSlot.Leader ("brak" always owned) limits LineStrength and replaces line visibility; LineData.Stretch adds to rod shock absorption, braid +10% hook-set; BaitData.AffinityAs/AffinityScale resolve via species' family keys (spinner/spoon/wobbler/softbait/fly, written into data/fish) when a species has no entry of its own (FishSpecies.AffinityFor, BaitFamilies); lures (Reusable) are not used per bite, are lost with the hook, and the bite timer runs only while reeling; FishSpecies.BiteOff = per-second chance to bite through a non-wire leader; accessories Waders (LakeBed.WadeLimit), HookHone, ReelGrease.
- Day 06-18 lasts 40 real minutes, night 20 (src/Core/DayClock); WorldState.TimeScale is the stool speed-up.
- 66 species in SpeciesCatalog (each needs LatinName, Description, a FishSkin style and an Abundance entry; GearSuite checks they are catchable).
- FightModel: the fish has position/heading/speed (burst speed from body length, limited turn rate), the taut line holds it at LineOut, it takes line only when it beats the drag; fights start with a strike run; drag-load drains stamina. Starter tackle is weak on purpose (junior rod 2.4 kg, reel drag 2 kg).
- Broken tackle is destroyed (Profile.Destroy: snapped rod, hook lost with a broken line, line lost when spooled). The tackle counter hands out the starter kit only when the missing pieces cost more than the purse (Profile.ClaimFreeKit, Shop free-kit button).
- HUD: minimap + clock top-right (UI/Minimap), fight bar under the status line (UI/TensionGauge). Fish atlas: UI/FishAtlas (menu and pause menu).

## Repository size
- The .glb models take ~350 MB for all 66 as committed (about 270 MB once rebuilt with the current WebP-85 encoding); main's history already holds every model version up to PR #5 (~1.2 GB of blobs, 742 MB on GitHub). Every full rebuild adds its whole size to the history: rebuild only the species that changed (`tools/fish_model/build_all.sh <ids>`), and before a branch with several full rebuilds is merged, keep only the final models in its history (rewrite the branch-only commits so that assets/fish/models appears in its last commit, then push with --force-with-lease).
